# 7-Day Trip Itinerary for India

| Day | Destination | Activities |
|-----|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Delhi | Explore Old Delhi, visit Red Fort, Jama Masjid, and Chandni Chowk market |
| 2 | Delhi → Agra | Visit the Taj Mahal at sunrise, Agra Fort, and Mehtab Bagh |
| 3 | Agra → Jaipur | Travel to Jaipur, visit City Palace and Jantar Mantar |
| 4 | Jaipur | Explore Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and shop at local bazaars |
| 5 | Jaipur → Udaipur | Travel to Udaipur, take a boat ride on Lake Pichola |
| 6 | Udaipur | Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, and Saheliyon-ki-Bari gardens |
| 7 | Udaipur → Delhi | Return to Delhi, last-minute shopping, and departure |
