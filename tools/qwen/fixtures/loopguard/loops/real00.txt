# Raymond III, Count of Tripoli

Raymond III (1140 – September 27 1187) was Count of Tripoli from 1152 to 1187 and Prince of Galilee twice through marriage. He was a major figure in the Kingdom of Jerusalem and served as regent for the kingdom twice. He was also known as Raymond II of Tripoli when he functioned as regent. He was the great-grandson of Raymond I of Toulouse and the great-great-grandson of Saint Gilles. His father was Alphonse I of Toulouse who was captured and died in captivity. Raymond succeeded his father as count of Tripoli in 1152 after his father was murdered by the Knights Templar in Jerusalem. Raymond was a highly educated man who spoke Arabic and was well informed about the affairs of the Islamic world.
